## Attacker path

Wrap a tiny amount of ETH, swap WETH→WBTC→DIGG to seed liquidity, create a DIGG/WETH SushiSwap pair with the resulting tokens, call sushiMaker.convert(WBTC, DIGG) which dumps maker WBTC through the attacker pair, then withdraw LP and route the residual DIGG back to WETH.

## Invariant broken

SushiMaker.convert routes fee tokens only through bridges it controls or can price safely.

## Victim impact

Attacker captured the WBTC fee accumulated in SushiMaker for the DIGG/WBTC pair via a single thinly-funded fake pool; historical writeups attribute the proceeds to the attacker's WETH wallet.

**Loss check:** WBTC fee balance held by SushiMaker (0xE11fc0B43ab98Eb91e9836129d1ee7c3Bc95df50) for the DIGG/WBTC pair drops to (near) zero post-convert.

## Root cause

SushiMaker burned LP fees and converted the resulting tokens to SUSHI via assumed bridge pairs, but DIGG/WBTC had no real bridge — convert(WBTC, DIGG) routed through any attacker-deployed DIGG/WETH pair without slippage protection.

**Protocol assumption that failed:** Assumed every onsen-listed pair had a configured bridge in SushiMaker before convert was callable.
