Deposit BPT (Balancer pool token 0x59686E01...) into Blacksmith (0xE0B94a7B...) via deposit(bpt, amount), then call claimRewards(bpt) — the bug mints an outsized COVER amount to the depositor.

## Invariant broken

Total COVER minted via Blacksmith equals the configured per-block reward rate times elapsed-blocks times pool-share.

## Victim impact

Effectively unbounded mint of COVER tokens against the staked BPT position; historical incident reported in the tens of millions before the white-hat reversal.

**Loss check:** COVER token totalSupply increases far beyond the protocol's per-block emissions schedule; staking pool reward index inconsistent with cumulative emissions.

## Root cause

Reward accounting in Blacksmith allowed claimRewards to mint COVER from a stale or unbounded reward index for the BPT pool, breaking the link between staked balance, reward rate, and minted supply.

**Protocol assumption that failed:** Assumed Blacksmith's reward index was monotonic and bounded by the pool's accumulated rewards.
