## Attacker path

Flash-loan reserves, call init again with attacker-controlled tokens, drain liquidity.

## Invariant broken

init runs exactly once and binds the pool's tokens for its lifetime.

## Victim impact

Approximately $3.8M extracted across multiple crowdpools.

**Loss check:** Crowdpool reserves < pre-attack reserves; reserve token now attacker-controlled.

## Root cause

init function lacked a guard against re-entry / re-initialization, and flash-loaned reserves left the legitimate state replaceable mid-call.

**Protocol assumption that failed:** Assumed init was guarded against re-entry once liquidity was present.
