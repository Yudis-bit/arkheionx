### Assertions added (proof target = exact base/quote token reconfiguration)

- `assertEq(dvmView._BASE_TOKEN_(),  address(wCRES_token))`
- `assertEq(dvmView._QUOTE_TOKEN_(), address(usdt_token))`

- `assertEq(dvmView._BASE_TOKEN_(),  token1)` — attacker-chosen base
- `assertEq(dvmView._QUOTE_TOKEN_(), token2)` — attacker-chosen quote
- `assertTrue(baseAfter  != baseBefore)`
- `assertTrue(quoteAfter != quoteBefore)`

These directly prove that re-`init` flipped both tokens from (wCRES, USDT) to attacker-controlled addresses, which is the F4 (unauthorized state transition) and F7 (control) requirement for the `initialization-bug` category.
