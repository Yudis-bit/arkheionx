## Victim impact

## Root cause

Oracle wrapper did not enforce freshness against the underlying Chainlink answer's updatedAt timestamp under the conditions present at the attack block.

**Protocol assumption that failed:** Assumed Chainlink answers were always fresh enough for instantaneous use.
