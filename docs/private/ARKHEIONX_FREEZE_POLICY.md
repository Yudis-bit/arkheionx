# Arkheionx Freeze Policy

Arkheionx V9.1 is frozen for real bounty usage for 2 weeks or 10 real bounty runs.

- No major new features.
- Only critical bugfixes proven by real runs.
- 80% bounty hunting, 20% maintenance.

During the freeze, record false positives, false negatives, parser limitations, and
decision-policy mistakes. Do not implement deferred architecture work until the freeze
condition is complete and the evidence from real runs has been reviewed.
